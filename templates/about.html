About The Project

My Project Overview

Strategic Product Placement Analysis is a data-driven project designed to identify the most effective product placement strategies within retail environments. By analyzing sales volume, customer demographics, foot traffic, product position, pricing, promotions, and competitor pricing, the project helps businesses optimize product visibility and maximize revenue.
